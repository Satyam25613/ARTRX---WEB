**Status:** internal working note, 14 November 2026
**Purpose:** map the 14 supplied image files to a cautious website placement decision.
**Publication:** the selected image is in the local gallery draft only. Permission to publish the photographs and artwork has not been confirmed here; do not deploy this image set until the relevant photo and artwork permissions are checked.

## Placement decision

One image is added to the Gallery page under **In progress**: `WhatsApp Image 2026-10-18 at 05.40.58 (2).jpeg`. It shows a hand drawing on a favorite-activity prompt sheet, with pencils nearby. The action makes it a better fit for “In progress” than for the homepage hero, a founder biography, or a completed-artwork section. Its caption describes only what can be seen and makes no claim about who took part or what the activity achieved.

The site uses a smaller, metadata-stripped copy of that photo. The original download is untouched. The card has a short, staggered entrance motion; visitors who prefer reduced motion do not see the animation.

## What this set can and cannot show

The photos show prompt sheets, pencils, drawing in progress, and people gathered around a table. That supports a careful description of the activity as creative work taking place. These images alone do not verify the program’s full delivery model, who is eligible, whether everyone pictured is a participant, or any health or emotional outcome. The website should not imply those things from a photograph.

Before publication, confirm who may approve the photographs and artwork, whether the people pictured agreed to public website use, whether any minors are included, and whether each drawing and any writing may be shown. If approval is unclear, keep the image off the live site. Avoid reproducing names, personal answers, or health details in captions and alt text.
